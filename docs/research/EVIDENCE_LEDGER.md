# Evidence ledger — azi-hub-v2

Ledger v1, opened 2026-11-15 with report R-01. One row per sourced claim. Research reports cite rows by ID. Rows are added, never renumbered; a row that turns out wrong is struck through and kept.

All rows below were gathered on 2026-11-15 for R-01.

## How to read a row

- **Read** says how the source was examined. `Code` = read in a clone of the repository at the commit shown. `Page` = the page was fetched and read. `Excerpt` = only the search-result excerpt of the page was read; the page itself was not opened.
- **Type**: `Primary` = the vendor's own code, documentation or announcement, or the body that produced the data. `Press` = independent news or analyst coverage. `Survey` = a named survey with a stated sample. `Vendor blog` = a company writing about a market it sells into, often about a competitor. `Blog` = an individual or small publication.
- **Conf.** is confidence that the claim is true as stated. `High` = primary source read directly. `Med` = independent press, a named survey, or several agreeing secondary sources. `Low` = one vendor blog, an estimate, an anecdote, or a source with a stake in the claim.
- **Bears on** lists the hypothesis (H-A1 to H-A4, V-000 section 5), capability (C-xx, R-01 section 4) or ranking (RANK, R-01 section 5) the row supports or weakens.
- Prices are list prices in US dollars unless stated, and change often. Re-check any price before relying on it.
