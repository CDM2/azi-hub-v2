CAT Research Source Manifest v2

Purpose

This manifest defines the provenance, role, and authority of the current
CAT research corpus in the Gemini Notebook.

The Notebook contains two distinct forms of research context:

1. Notebook Sources — files and synthesized artifacts
intentionally preserved as part of the active research corpus.
2. Notebook Conversations — historical or working Gemini chat
threads that preserve provenance, reasoning history, and context but
should not automatically be treated as canonical research
conclusions.

Where a synthesized artifact exists as a Notebook Source, that artifact
is the durable output of the corresponding working conversation and
should be the preferred reference for subsequent phases.

Part III — Corpus Authority and Provenance Rules

1. Historical Material Is Evidence, Not Specification

The AZI Hub Project Brief and Historical Reference Conversation document
prior implementation experience and prior conceptual thinking. They may
contain valuable hypotheses and empirical lessons, but neither defines
the final CAT product.

2. Working Plans Are Methodology, Not Market Evidence

CAT_Research_to_Product_Plan and To Do List - Agentic OS (CAT)
define the current research process and intended execution sequence.
They may be revised as the research program becomes better specified.

3. Canonical Synthesized Artifacts Govern Subsequent Research

Where a completed research conversation has produced an approved durable
artifact, subsequent phases should preferentially use the approved
artifact:

00_FOUNDER_CORPUS_MAP_v1.md → 01_CORPUS_BLIND_SPOT_AUDIT_v1.md →
02_RESEARCH_PROBLEM_v2.md

Future approved research artifacts should extend this chain.

4. Conversations Preserve Provenance

Notebook Conversations remain available because they preserve reasoning
history, historical context, intermediate drafts, and the path by which
durable artifacts were created. They should not be treated as equivalent
in authority to an approved canonical artifact when the two conflict.

5. Superseded Drafts Should Not Become Competing Sources

Intermediate drafts produced inside conversations do not need to be
added as Notebook Sources. If an approved artifact supersedes an earlier
draft, only the approved artifact should remain in the active source
corpus unless there is a specific provenance reason to preserve the
earlier version.

6. Uncertainty Must Be Preserved

No source or conversation in the current corpus establishes that CAT
should exist as a distinct product category; that "Agentic OS" is the
correct category; that any particular control point or architecture is
correct; that any Greenfield/Brownfield or Single-Org/Multi-Org quadrant
is the correct wedge; or that any specific business model or moat is
viable.
